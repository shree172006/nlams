Statutory Mathematical Engine

First Schedule Algorithmic Compensation Formulation

Slide 4 / 8

Gross Compensation = Base Compensation (C_Base) + 100% Solatium + 12% Additional Amount

  • • Sec 26 Base Rate: Max(Stamp Act Circle Rate, Top 50% 3-Year Registered Sale Deeds, Consented PPP Rate)
  • • Sec 26(2) Factor: Urban = 1.0x | Rural Close = 1.25x | Rural Mid = 1.50x | Rural Remote = 2.00x
  • • Sec 29 Assets: Structural Valuation + Standing Trees + Standing Crops
  • • Sec 30(1) Solatium: Mandatory 100% on (Adjusted Land Value + Assets)
  • • Sec 30(3) Additional Amount: 12% p.a. on Base Market Value from Sec 11 Notification to Award Date

Compliance & Rigor

Key Judge Q&A Defenses (Pitcher Strategy)

Slide 6 / 8
Q: Land is a State subject. How does a Central portal ensure compliance?

A: RFCTLARR Act 2013 is under Concurrent List (Entry 42). Central agencies fund 100% of compensation. NLAMS integrates with state land portals via open APIs and incentivizes states via 140-Point Vikas Scores.

Q: How does NLAMS eliminate financial corruption?

A: PFMS Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) routes funds directly from Central Ministry accounts to verified landholder bank accounts via APBS, eliminating intermediate CALA bank floats.

Q: How do you handle disputes without stalling national projects?

A: Section 64 reference mechanism deposits disputed funds into Civil Court / LARR Authority escrow, allowing physical possession (Sec 38) to proceed without delaying national corridor construction.
